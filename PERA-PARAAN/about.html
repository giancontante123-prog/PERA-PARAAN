HOW IT BEGAN

Our Story

Our story began when we were freshmen at PUP. Ayesha Vallejos turned her love of stickers and creative design into a small business, starting with an idea and the courage to share it online. Students connected with the designs and showed us that something made with care could brighten their school days and express what they love.

As more students discovered Pera-Paraan, we wanted them to see the products up close, so we brought our stickers to PUP Lagoon and joined freshman events and PUP SINTA. Meeting people in person, hearing their ideas, and seeing which designs made them smile gave us the confidence to keep going. With every event, we learned to improve our products, try new ideas, and make the experience more personal.

What started as Ayesha's freshman-year project has grown into something we build together. As founder and co-founder, we continue to create affordable, thoughtful designs for the PUP community, one sticker and one new connection at a time.

WHAT WE DO

Our Mission

To create thoughtful, affordable designs that bring personality to everyday student life and help people celebrate meaningful moments.

WHERE WE'RE GOING

Our Vision

To grow into a trusted creative brand known for personal, joyful, and accessible anik-anik for PUP students.
